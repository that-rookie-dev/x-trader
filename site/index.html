02 / The loop

It learns. You stay in control.

Every whitelisted symbol gets its own forecast parameters and history. The app resolves predictions after market close, measures error, and tunes the next session.

Session-aware training

Closed-loop forecasting, without the mystery.

Live scan cycles run during market hours. After close, one tune cycle resolves forecasts against actual closes and then stops at 100%.

  • Per-symbol ALGO and AI parameter deltas
  • MAE, hit-rate, samples, and prediction ledger
  • Visible cycle progress and clear closure
  • Archived history for removed symbols

05 / FAQ

Straight answers.

Does xTrader place live orders?

No. It is an analysis and instruction desk. Zerodha access is read-only, and broker order adapters refuse placement. You execute trades yourself.

Where is my data stored?

On your machine under ~/.xtrader. The bundle includes PostgreSQL and binds it to loopback only.

Do I need Node or Docker?

No Docker. If Node 20+ is missing, the installer downloads an official local Node runtime into the xTrader install directory.

How do updates work?

When a newer GitHub Release exists, xTrader shows an in-app update banner. Update & restart preserves your database and configuration.

What gets trained?

Only F&O symbols on your Settings whitelist. Removed symbols disappear from current UI metrics, but their historical data stays local for future use.
