01 / The desk

Know what matters before you act.

The horizon you pick leads the desk. News comes next: the latest read on top, and one delta that can tighten both forecasts when a model is connected. Everything else — your list, your broker picture, the daily review — stays in the same calm workspace.

01LEADS THE DESK

Pick how far ahead

Seven horizons run together: 5 minutes, 15, 30, 1 hour, 4 hours, 6 hours, and the cash close. Switch one, and the option chain follows it. The chart keeps only two lines — the live price, and that horizon’s prediction — and both move every second. A short horizon keeps an option’s time value. The close is the close.

02SAME ON BOTH

One news delta

Every fifteen minutes, the model asks whether the world can move each name you follow. The answer becomes points on the predicted close, added equally to the rule-based forecast and the AI forecast, on every horizon. When a story can move that name, the close moves with it. That is how the read improves accuracy. The horizon you picked does not resize the shift.

03OPTIONAL

Off means unchanged

A connected model can tighten the call. With no model, or if the model drops out, the news pass pauses. Price, levels, and the rest keep their full weight, so their accuracy stays as it is. A missing delta does not stand in and pull the forecast down.

04READ-ONLY

The news timeline

Open News for one symbol from your watchlist. The latest read sits at the top, with a line between each pass. Every story shows its preview and a short line: whether it affected that symbol, and how. Delta and score sit above the thread. Nothing there places a trade. With no model connected, the page says the delta is not active.

05ALGO / AI

Two forecast views

Compare the rule-based forecast with the AI-assisted view. They can disagree on the path. They always share the same news shift, or no news shift at all.

06YOUR BROKER

Your Zerodha picture

Funds, holdings, positions, and completed orders sit in the same view. Zerodha access stays read-only. Live orders remain yours.

07YOUR LIST

Only the names you choose

Indices and F&O stocks you add are the only names xTrader follows, scans, and learns from. Remove one from today, and its past history stays on your computer.

08YOUR MODEL

Connect a model to turn news on

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, and others work, as do Ollama and LM Studio on your own computer. The forecast runs either way. The news delta waits until a model is active.

02 / The loop

It learns from each market day.

During the session, xTrader remembers the live market picture behind each forecast. After the close, it checks what really happened and looks for small ways to improve.

A daily improvement cycle

The day ends. The learning cycle does too.

The cycle does not run forever. It checks the day’s forecasts, tests small per-symbol adjustments, keeps only changes that improve past results, reaches 100%, and waits for the next session. It does not retrain the AI model itself.

Paper Autopilot can rehearse the fifteen-minute view on its own: one long idea, a tight loss limit, and an exit when that horizon arrives. The practice stays on paper.

05 / FAQ

Straight answers.

Does xTrader place live orders?

No. Zerodha access is read-only, and you place every live trade yourself. Paper Autopilot can rehearse an idea on paper, with loss limits, and that rehearsal never becomes a broker order.

Where are my settings and history kept?

They stay on your computer, including your watchlist, forecasts, outcomes, and learning history. Your access details are protected.

Which lookahead can I follow?

Five minutes, fifteen, thirty, one hour, four hours, six hours, and the cash close. All of them are computed. The one you select drives the option chain and the chart. The chart shows the live price and that prediction, updated every second.

What happens to forecasts if I have no AI model?

They keep running on price, levels, and the other inputs, at the same strength. The news pass stays paused, and News shows that the delta is not active. Connect a model when you want that fifteen-minute read. A missing delta leaves the accuracy of those other inputs unchanged.

How does the news delta change a prediction?

It adds the same points to the Algo close and the AI close, on every horizon. A positive delta lifts the predicted price. A negative delta pulls it down. When the read finds news that can move that symbol, the forecast moves with it, which is how accuracy improves. Hover the delta or the score on the News page to see the same explanation. Each story also carries a short line on whether it affected the symbol, and how.

Can I choose which AI model to use?

Yes. You can connect paid providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, and others. You can also run models on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio.

Does the daily learning run forever?

No. After the market closes, xTrader checks the day’s forecasts, tests small improvements, reaches 100%, and waits for the next session. It does not retrain the AI model itself.

How do updates work?

When a newer version is available, xTrader shows an in-app alert. Choose Update & Restart and the app handles the rest while keeping your settings and history.

What happens when I remove a symbol?

It disappears from current tracking and learning screens. Its past history stays on your computer, ready if you choose that symbol again later.
